## 1. Scope and method

- [PUBLISHED EVIDENCE][NATIONAL] Scope of this note: Poshan Tracker (the Government of India ICT platform for Anganwadi/AWC service delivery), PMMVY, and central-government digital resources relevant to a Telugu-first voice reporting **research prototype**. No official integration, submission, credential use, or beneficiary data access was attempted.
- [PUBLISHED EVIDENCE] Method: direct retrieval of official pages (poshantracker.in, spniwcd.wcd.gov.in, wcd.gov.in, pmmvy.wcd.gov.in, web.umang.gov.in, poshanabhiyaan.gov.in), an official PDF circular, one app-store listing, and search-engine result pages. Pages that block direct HTTP access were read through the `r.jina.ai` reader proxy; this is a transport workaround, not a different source.
- [PUBLISHED EVIDENCE] Search limitation: the built-in `websearch` tool failed with HTTP 429/402 throughout the session. Search was therefore attempted via Bing web/news, DuckDuckGo (bot challenge), Google (`sorry/index` bot block) and Brave (429/CAPTCHA). Results were often generic entity results, so **search silence is not proof of absence** — it is labelled `[UNKNOWN]` below.
- [PUBLISHED EVIDENCE] Source preference applied: official MWCD/SPNIWCD/POSHAN Tracker material > UMANG (Government of India aggregator) > credible news > app-store. App-store descriptions and reviews are **not** treated as proof of actual worker workflow.
- [UNVERIFIED] No field observation, worker interview, or account walkthrough was performed in this research task; all workflow claims below are documentary.

## 2. Poshan Tracker — official picture

### 2.1 What it is and who owns it

- [VERIFIED][NATIONAL] Poshan Tracker is a Government of India digital platform under the **Ministry of Women and Child Development (MWCD)**: the official site's structured metadata names MWCD as author/publisher, and the Google Play listing names the developer as "National eGovernance Division, Government of India". Sources: (1) `https://www.poshantracker.in/` (JSON-LD `WebApplication`, author MWCD); (2) `https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.poshantracker&hl=en-US`. Publication date: not stated on either page; access date 2026-11-16. Note: the two sources are different publishers but describe the same official product.
- [PUBLISHED EVIDENCE][NATIONAL] It is described by MWCD's Savitribai Phule National Institute of Women and Child Development (SPNIWCD) as "an ICT based governance tool under Saksham Anganwadi and Mission Poshan 2.0", "rolled out on 1st March 2021 to monitor progress of Mission Poshan 2.0 in near real-time basis". Source: SPNIWCD, *Poshan Tracker — Brief*, `https://www.spniwcd.wcd.gov.in/poshan-tracker`, date not stated (page footer "Last updated 11/16/26"), access 2026-11-16.
- [PUBLISHED EVIDENCE][NATIONAL] The same rollout timing appears on the official product site: the "Poshan Tracker Journey" marks **March 2021 — Nationwide Rollout**, with "Integration with existing schemes and platforms for seamless data flow and analysis" and large-scale AWW training. Source: MWCD, *About Poshan Tracker*, `https://www.poshantracker.in/aboutus`, date not stated (footer "Last updated: 13/11/2026"), access 2026-11-16. **Caution:** both date sources belong to the MWCD family, so this is treated as one credible publisher, not two independent ones.
- [PUBLISHED EVIDENCE][NATIONAL] Stated objectives (SPNIWCD brief): dynamic identification of **stunting, wasting and under-weight** prevalence among children; last-mile tracking of nutrition service delivery; digitisation and automation of the physical registers used by the Anganwadi Worker. Source: SPNIWCD brief (above).
- [PUBLISHED EVIDENCE][NATIONAL] Product-site framing: a "job-aid to the Anganwadi worker for efficient delivery of services", a "beneficiary-centric service delivery Application under POSHAN Abhiyaan", and a source of "real time data with analytics to supervisors". Source: MWCD, *About Poshan Tracker* (above).
- [PUBLISHED EVIDENCE][NATIONAL] Scale figures (official, historical): 13.9 lakh AWCs onboarded and geo-tagging/dashboard/multi-lingual features introduced in 2022; 10+ crore beneficiaries and 45,527 state officials in 2023; as on March 2024 — 97.37% Aadhaar-verified beneficiaries, 84,244,752 children (0–6 years), 96.92% home visits against 23,112,638 target visits, 31,983,452 THR and 37,35,981 HCM distributions. Source: MWCD, *About Poshan Tracker* (above). These are programme-level claims, not worker-level workflow evidence.
- [PUBLISHED EVIDENCE][NATIONAL] An official MWCD circular dated **10 April 2023** records "around 13.9 lakh AWCs have started using the ICT Application" and "9.9 Crore beneficiaries have been registered under Poshan Tracker (as in February 2023)", with >90% Aadhaar-verified. Source: MWCD, *Monitoring last-mile delivery of Supplementary Nutrition through Poshan Tracker — regarding*, F.No. PA/22/2021-CPMU (e-91231), `https://www.spniwcd.wcd.gov.in/uploads/pdf/1706599962_74LZmKpvuW.pdf`, published 2023-04-10, access 2026-11-16.
- [PUBLISHED EVIDENCE][NATIONAL] Scheme context: Anganwadi Services (formerly ICDS, launched 1975) has been revised and subsumed under **Mission Saksham Anganwadi & Poshan 2.0**, which includes supplementary nutrition, pre-school non-formal education, nutrition & health education, immunisation, health check-up and referral services; immunisation, health check-up and referral are delivered by NHM/public health infrastructure. Source: SPNIWCD, *Mission Saksham Anganwadi and Poshan 2.0*, `https://www.spniwcd.wcd.gov.in/mission-saksham-anganwadi-and-poshan-2-0`, date not stated, access 2026-11-16.

### 2.2 Modules and user roles

- [PUBLISHED EVIDENCE][NATIONAL] Intended app users, per the official FAQ: "Anganwadi Workers, Supervisors, CDPOs, DPOs, and Poshan Tracker beneficiaries (for existing beneficiary services and new registrations)". Dashboard access is "only [for] nominated Nodal level officials at National, State, District, Project and Sector level" with unique credentials. Sources: MWCD, *Poshan Tracker FAQ*, `https://www.poshantracker.in/faq`, date not stated (page metadata 13 Nov 2026), access 2026-11-16; see also `https://admin.poshantracker.in/` (Department Login, linked from the official site).
- [PUBLISHED EVIDENCE][NATIONAL] Six beneficiary categories an AWW can register: Pregnant Women; Lactating Mothers; children 0–6 months; children 6 months–3 years; children 3–6 years; Adolescent Girls 14–18 years (**only in Aspirational Districts**). Source: Poshan Tracker FAQ (above).
- [PUBLISHED EVIDENCE][NATIONAL] The **Action Centre** consolidates frequently used functions: Register Beneficiary, Verify Aadhaar, Upcoming Beneficiary Changes, Stock Register, **Referral Register**, Identify Father/Guardian Aadhaar, Beneficiary Vaccination, Migrated Out Beneficiaries, Family Survey. Source: Poshan Tracker FAQ (above).
- [PUBLISHED EVIDENCE][NATIONAL] Module evolution published by MWCD: **2021** — beneficiary profiles, Home Visit Scheduler, beneficiary migration, orphan children, AWC status/AWC infra; **2022** — Daily Tracking with offline feature, internal dashboard, multi-lingual, improved home page/Action Centre, beneficiary redressal, Mission Utkarsh dashboard; **2023** — THR/HCM module in Daily Tracking, MPR, AWW dashboard, AWC location and photo capture, RCH/UWIN integration through ABHA, heat map, public dashboard; **2024** — assisted home visits, 8 registers digitised, search-equipped AWC migration, AWW/AWH e-KYC, child growth history with chart, growth trends dashboard, stock entry register, beneficiary self-registration, gestational weight gain. Source: MWCD, *About Poshan Tracker* (above).
- [PUBLISHED EVIDENCE][NATIONAL] The application is Android-only (Android 6.0+), available in **24 languages including Telugu**, with offline capability limited to the Daily Tracking module storing up to **5 days** of data. Source: Poshan Tracker FAQ (above).
- [PUBLISHED EVIDENCE][NATIONAL] Reporting cadence in the official FAQ: current-month reports show data up to the previous day (AWC Daily Monitoring from previous day onward; offline data visible after five days); monthly reports show the previous month; app data appears on the dashboard after one day. Source: Poshan Tracker FAQ (above).
- [PUBLISHED EVIDENCE][NATIONAL] Performance/incentive thresholds stated in the FAQ: AWWs need at least **60% of targeted home visits** (PW, LM, children 0–2 years) and **80% of growth monitoring** of children 0–6; AWH incentive requires the AWC to be open at least 21 days. Source: Poshan Tracker FAQ (above).
- [UNVERIFIED][NATIONAL] The Google Play description ("360-degree view of the activities of AWCs, service deliveries of AWWs, and complete beneficiary management…") corroborates the official purpose but is an app-store product description and is **not** used here as proof of actual worker workflow. Source: Google Play listing (above), updated 2026-10-22, access 2026-11-16.

### 2.3 Reported user workflow (source by source)

- [PUBLISHED EVIDENCE][NATIONAL] **Daily attendance / AWC status** — In the *Daily Tracking* module the AWW marks AWC open/closed status, children's attendance, morning snacks, HCM, ECCE learning modules, and preschool education/personal-hygiene activities. Attendance applies to children 3–6 years who attend the AWC; the module is restricted on Sundays; attendance can be changed until 9:59 pm the same day; once AWW status is marked it cannot be changed. Source: Poshan Tracker FAQ (above).
- [PUBLISHED EVIDENCE][NATIONAL] **Growth monitoring** — Children 0–6 years (and adolescent girls 14–18 in Aspirational Districts) are eligible; growth/health details appear in the *Care Profile* of the beneficiary card; SAM and SUW children can be measured four times a month, others once a month; a Supervisor can correct incorrect entries the next day; WHO growth charts are provided in-app/on the website; colour coding: Red = SAM, Yellow = MAM, Brown = Overweight, Blue = Obese, Green = Normal; SAM/underweight lists appear on the AWW dashboard. Source: Poshan Tracker FAQ (above).
- [PUBLISHED EVIDENCE][NATIONAL] **Home visits** — Routine AWW visits for counselling, follow-up, behaviour change, awareness and community support; eligible beneficiaries: pregnant women, lactating mothers, children up to 2 years, and malnourished children; the AWW sees Completed/Due/Missed records with counts; the 60% threshold applies to the performance incentive; a visit cannot be marked after its time window has passed. Source: Poshan Tracker FAQ (above). An official learning video "Making or Updating Home Visit Planner and Initiating home visits" and a PDF "General Awareness about Home Visit Scheduler" are hosted in the Resources Centre (see §5).
- [PUBLISHED EVIDENCE][NATIONAL] **Meal/nutrition (THR)** — Step-by-step THR distribution documented by the FAQ: login → Daily Tracking → THR/HCM → choose category (PW / LM / children 6m–3y / adolescent girls) → search beneficiary → tap THR → select number of days and number of packets → choose **Face Verification** under Verification Method → capture and verify face → Proceed. The FAQ also states Aadhaar is mandatory for THR (3-month grace period for beneficiaries registered without it), a nominee (≥18 years, added with consent OTP and face capture) may collect THR, and THR may be given to children based on the mother's/father's/guardian's face authentication with **no child photograph required**. Source: Poshan Tracker FAQ (above).
- [PUBLISHED EVIDENCE][NATIONAL] **Meal/nutrition (HCM)** — Hot Cooked Meal is for children 3–6 years who visit the AWC, and "in some states" is provided to all beneficiary categories under state-specific provisions. Source: Poshan Tracker FAQ (above).
- [PUBLISHED EVIDENCE][NATIONAL] **Supplementary nutrition last-mile tracking** — MWCD's 10 April 2023 circular directs States/UTs to track receipt of THR by Aadhaar-verified beneficiaries through **serialised QR codes** and an **SMS alert/OTP** system; AWWs and beneficiaries scan the QR code to see THR composition, date of manufacture and nutrition content; QR coding and SMS alerts are stated to be mandatory for THR under Mission Poshan 2.0. **Caution:** this is a directive to States, not independent confirmation that every AWC performs these scans. Source: MWCD circular (above), 2023-04-10.
- [PUBLISHED EVIDENCE][NATIONAL] **Referrals** — The official FAQ lists a *Referral Register* among Action Centre functions; SPNIWCD states referral services are one of the six Anganwadi Services packages and are delivered through NHM/public health infrastructure. Source: Poshan Tracker FAQ and SPNIWCD Mission Poshan 2.0 page (both above).
- [UNKNOWN][NATIONAL] No official source retrieved describes the detailed referral *workflow* in Poshan Tracker (who initiates, what fields are recorded, where the referral is sent, whether it is a hand-off to NHM). The Referral Register's existence is documented; its procedure is not. Searched: official FAQ, resources, SPNIWCD documents, general web.
- [PUBLISHED EVIDENCE][NATIONAL] **Other recorded events** — The "More" section lets AWWs record three events: VHSND, CBE (twice monthly) and AWW Training; there are 10 training modules; a "Raise Issue" option emails issues to the National Poshan Support Team; AWC location can be mapped via "Update AWC Location" only when physically present at the AWC. Source: Poshan Tracker FAQ (above).
- [PUBLISHED EVIDENCE][NATIONAL] **Stock and migration** — Stock Register appears in the Action Centre; 2024 introduced a Stock Entry Register; beneficiary migration between AWCs (within/outside state, district, project, sector) is performed by the **destination AWW** using the beneficiary's verified mobile number and Aadhaar; category migration (e.g., PW → LM after delivery date, including abortion/miscarriage/stillbirth options) is separately documented. Source: Poshan Tracker FAQ and About page (both above).
- [UNKNOWN][STATE-AP] No Andhra Pradesh-specific central document was found that describes how these workflows are localised (e.g., AP-specific HCM eligibility or language packs). Do not assume AP behaviour from national text.

### 2.4 Photo / document upload (if documented)

- [PUBLISHED EVIDENCE][NATIONAL] **Face Recognition System (FRS)** is the documented photographic mechanism. Step 1 (one-time e-KYC): AWW enters Aadhaar number and registered mobile number, an OTP completes e-KYC, and **a live photo of the beneficiary is captured and matched with the Aadhaar photo** at the back end. Step 2 (monthly or each THR collection): a fresh photo is captured and matched against the stored verified image; face matching works offline. Source: Poshan Tracker FAQ (above).
- [PUBLISHED EVIDENCE][NATIONAL] FRS scope limits stated officially: children below 6 years do **not** need FRS for THR; no photograph of the child is required; a nominee undergoes FRS; on failure the AWW should request re-capture, call helpline **1515** (earlier 14408), or inform Supervisor/CDPO. Safeguards stated: consent before e-KYC/FRS, authorised-access only, images cannot be copied/misused, and **no photos or data are permanently stored on mobile devices** (temporary data deleted after logout); beneficiary data is stated to be protected under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Source: Poshan Tracker FAQ (above).
- [PUBLISHED EVIDENCE][NATIONAL] An official awareness PDF "FRS (English)" / "FRS (Hindi)" is hosted in the Resources Centre, confirming FRS is an published, trained workflow rather than an undocumented feature. Source: MWCD, *Resource Center*, `https://www.poshantracker.in/resources`, `https://cdn.poshantracker.in/public-assets/pdf/Resources/Awareness/FRS_en.pdf`, date not stated, access 2026-11-16.
- [PUBLISHED EVIDENCE][NATIONAL] "AWC Location and Photo Capture" is listed as a 2023 core-module introduction, and geo-tagging is described as associating coordinates with interventions/beneficiaries/service points, with AWWs prompted to update AWC locations and Supervisors verifying. Source: MWCD, *About Poshan Tracker* (above).
- [UNKNOWN][NATIONAL] No official documentation was found for **generic document upload** (e.g., uploading ration cards, birth certificates, MCP cards as files) inside Poshan Tracker. The documented image capture is for FRS identity verification and AWC location/photo; do not infer a general document store.
- [UNVERIFIED][NATIONAL] App-store reviews (Nov 2026) report face-capture/recognition failures, app crashes, missing auto-save, and handset overheating during photo capture. These are user reports, not verified workflow facts. The developer's public reply directs users to a weekday VC session and to Supervisor/CDPO for device issues. Source: Google Play listing (above), access 2026-11-16.

## 3. PMMVY — relevance assessment

- [VERIFIED][NATIONAL] **What it is:** Pradhan Mantri Matru Vandana Yojana is a **Centrally Sponsored maternity-benefit scheme of MWCD**, launched pan-India with effect from **01.01.2017**, implementing Section 4 of the National Food Security Act, 2013: cash incentive ₹5,000 for the first child (₹3,000 + ₹2,000 instalments) via DBT, and ₹6,000 for a second child **if that child is a girl**, with average ₹6,000 including JSY convergence. Sources: (1) MWCD, *Pradhan Mantri Matru Vandana Yojana*, `https://wcd.gov.in/offerings/pradhan-mantri-matru-vandana-yojana`; (2) Government of India, *UMANG — Pradhan Mantri Matru Vandana Yojana*, `https://web.umang.gov.in/landing/scheme/detail/pradhan-matri-matra-vandana-yojana_pmmvy.html`. Both accessed 2026-11-16; publication dates not stated on the pages.
- [PUBLISHED EVIDENCE][NATIONAL] PMMVY 2.0 under **Mission Shakti / Samarthya** applies with effect from **01.04.2022**; the official FAQ explicitly corrects a common misconception: "PMMVY is not part of umbrella ICDS… after FY 2021-22, the scheme is under Samarthya of Mission Shakti." Source: MWCD PMMVY official page (above).
- [PUBLISHED EVIDENCE][NATIONAL] **Who uses it:** eligible pregnant/lactating women (18 years 7 months–55 years at childbirth, socially/economically disadvantaged criteria), applying within 270 days of childbirth through the PMMVY portal (`https://pmmvy.wcd.gov.in`) or mobile app; AWWs and ASHA workers can fill the form on a beneficiary's behalf and monitor status. Implemented by State WCD/Social Welfare departments (Health Departments in Gujarat, Tamil Nadu, West Bengal). Source: MWCD PMMVY official page (above).
- [PUBLISHED EVIDENCE][NATIONAL] AWWs have a defined **assistive** role and are paid an honorarium for online applications: ₹150 (first instalment) + ₹100 (second instalment) for the first child, ₹250 for a second girl child, and ₹150 for field verification in self-registration cases. A pregnant/lactating AWW/AWH may herself be an eligible beneficiary. Source: MWCD PMMVY official page (above).
- [PUBLISHED EVIDENCE][NATIONAL] **Actual relevance to routine Anganwadi activity reporting:** PMMVY is a **separate DBT maternity-payment system with its own portal, app, eligibility rules, conditionalities and MIS**. Its connection to AWC routine work is limited to (a) AWW/ASHA assistance with PMMVY applications, (b) receipt of a Poshan Tracker-derived "Due List" of potential beneficiaries, and (c) reuse of Poshan Tracker's FRS for identity verification. It is **not** a tool for reporting growth monitoring, attendance, THR/HCM, or home visits. Source: MWCD PMMVY official page (above).
- [UNKNOWN][NATIONAL] No credible source was found stating that PMMVY data is written back into routine ICDS/AWC service-delivery reporting, or that PMMVY replaces any Poshan Tracker reporting module. Do not assume bidirectional sync.
- [PUBLISHED EVIDENCE][NATIONAL] PMMVY is implemented in "35 States/UTs except Telangana" per the official FAQ; Telangana runs its own maternity benefit scheme. The source does not name Andhra Pradesh individually. Source: MWCD PMMVY official page (above).
- [UNKNOWN][STATE-AP] No AP-specific PMMVY operational document was retrieved in this task. Do not assume the "Due List" or FRS reuse is live in AP without field confirmation.
- [PUBLISHED EVIDENCE][NATIONAL] Difference from similarly named schemes: **PM POSHAN** (Pradhan Mantri Poshan Shakti Nirman, mid-day meal in schools) is a Ministry of Education scheme and is **not** PMMVY or Poshan Tracker. Source: `https://pmposhan.education.gov.in/index.html`, surfaced in search results 2026-11-16. This distinction matters to avoid conflating "Poshan" schemes.

## 4. Documented relationships and integrations (central)

Only explicitly documented relationships are listed. No inference from forum chatter, app-store text, or user statements.

- [PUBLISHED EVIDENCE][NATIONAL] **Poshan Tracker → PMMVY "Due List" (explicit data transfer):** the official WCD FAQ states the Ministry "is now using the available database of the Poshan Tracker to create a 'Due List' of potential beneficiaries. All such pregnant and lactating mothers registered on the Poshan Tracker and meeting certain criteria are included in this list. **The list is then transferred from the Poshan Tracker to the PMMVY portal at the Anganwadi level.** This list is accessible to Anganwadi Workers (AWWs) and ASHA workers." A separate FAQ answer says "Available database of PW&LM from Poshan Tracker is used to create a 'Due List'…". Source: MWCD PMMVY official page (above). Only one official publisher was found, so this is `[PUBLISHED EVIDENCE]`, not `[VERIFIED]`.
- [PUBLISHED EVIDENCE][NATIONAL] **Poshan Tracker FRS reused by PMMVY (explicit shared component):** "Mandatory biometric verification through facial authentication has been introduced from **May 21, 2025**, for all new enrolments under PMMVY. **The existing Facial Recognition System (FRS) available on the Poshan Tracker is being used for this purpose**, thus eliminating the need for duplicate verification procedures. In case the FRS on Poshan Tracker is unavailable, an additional feature of UIDAI-based facial authentication through the Aadhaar FaceRD app is also available." The WCD PMMVY documents list also includes "Letter dated 19.06.2025 to States/UTs regarding Facial Recognition under PMMVY — Annexure-V". Source: MWCD PMMVY official page (above).
- [PUBLISHED EVIDENCE][NATIONAL] **RCH → Poshan Tracker (explicit data fetch):** the official FAQ states "The data is fetched from the **RCH System** and is reflected in the respective categories of the Poshan Tracker Application", with an RCH Profile visible for pregnant women and lactating mothers (requiring mobile verification, Aadhaar verification, ABHA ID and ABHA Address). Source: Poshan Tracker FAQ (above).
- [PUBLISHED EVIDENCE][NATIONAL] **RCH / ABHA / UWIN / APAAR / LGD (explicit platform claims):** the About page lists "Integration with healthcare platforms like RCH, ABHA, and UWIN through ABHA" as a 2023 module, integration with **APAAR** to link a child's Anganwadi record to school education, and **LGD compliance** for administrative hierarchies. Sources: MWCD, *About Poshan Tracker* and *Poshan Tracker FAQ* (both above). No technical interface specification is published.
- [PUBLISHED EVIDENCE][NATIONAL] **Programme hierarchy, not a data sync:** Poshan Tracker sits under Mission Saksham Anganwadi & Poshan 2.0; Anganwadi Services (formerly ICDS) has been subsumed into that Mission; Poshan Abhiyaan (launched by the Prime Minister on 8 March 2018) is a component. Sources: SPNIWCD, *Poshan Tracker — Brief* and *Mission Saksham Anganwadi and Poshan 2.0* (both above).
- [PUBLISHED EVIDENCE][NATIONAL] **PMMVY-CAS is a distinct system:** WCD's PMMVY documents list "PMMVY-CAS User Manual (September 2017)", "Training… on use of PMMVY-CAS", and RFPs for "PMMVY-CAS" IT infrastructure. It is the case-management system for PMMVY, not the ICDS Common Application Software. Source: MWCD PMMVY official page (above).
- [PUBLISHED EVIDENCE][NATIONAL] **ICDS-CAS → Poshan Tracker (reviewer-reconciled):** the World Bank 2024 learning note (source 8 in `ICDS_REPORTING_REQUIREMENTS.md`) explicitly describes ICDS-CAS being replaced by Poshan Tracker; Shukla & Kapur 2022 and the MWCD guidelines corroborate the transition. No MWCD/PIB page retrieved here states it, which is why this file originally marked it `[UNKNOWN]`. **Replacement is published evidence; data migration, shared databases and API sync remain `[UNKNOWN]` — do not infer them.**
- [UNKNOWN][NATIONAL/STATE-AP] **Bala Sanjeevini:** could not establish what this system/programme is, which authority owns it, or any relationship to Poshan Tracker, ICDS or Anganwadi reporting. Searches (Bing web, Bing News, Google, Brave; several phrasings including "Bala Sanjeevini Anganwadi Andhra Pradesh", "Bala Sanjeevini ICDS Anganwadi scheme") returned either unrelated consumer results or bot blocks. No credible source found — treat the name as unverified until field observation identifies it.
- [UNKNOWN][NATIONAL] No source found documenting a Poshan Tracker integration with **DigiLocker**, **UMANG**, or a public "beneficiary passbook" release; the About page mentions an "upcoming beneficiary passbook feature", which is not evidence of a live integration. Source: MWCD, *About Poshan Tracker* (above).

## 6. Contradictions, outdated documentation and cautions

- [PUBLISHED EVIDENCE][NATIONAL] **Internal inconsistency in the official FAQ on THR days:** one answer states a maximum of **25 days** of THR, while the nominee section states a beneficiary is entitled to a maximum of **30 days per month** and that split collections (e.g., 15 + 15) total 30. Both appear in the same FAQ; do not hard-code either limit into a prototype without field confirmation. Source: Poshan Tracker FAQ (above), access 2026-11-16.
- [PUBLISHED EVIDENCE][NATIONAL] **Different beneficiary counts, different dates/scopes:** MWCD circular (Feb 2023) reports 9.9 crore registered beneficiaries; About page reports 10+ crore in 2023 and 84,244,752 children (0–6) as on March 2024. These are likely different measures/time points, not necessarily a conflict, but they should not be mixed. Sources: MWCD circular and About page (above).
- [PUBLISHED EVIDENCE][NATIONAL] **Helpline number changed:** FAQ says "POSHAN Helpline 1515 (earlier 14408)". Older training material or state circulars quoting 14408 are likely superseded. Source: Poshan Tracker FAQ (above).
- [PUBLISHED EVIDENCE][NATIONAL] **PMMVY 1.0 vs 2.0:** instalment structure, eligibility criteria and scheme home changed from 01.04.2022 (ICDS umbrella → Mission Shakti/Samarthya; three instalments → two; ₹6,000 second-girl-child benefit added). Pre-2022 documents on the WCD page (2017–2021) are superseded for current rules. Source: MWCD PMMVY official page (above).
- [PUBLISHED EVIDENCE][NATIONAL] **Rapidly evolving app:** SPNIWCD document list carries app/dashboard enhancement letters dated 14 Feb 2024 and 19 June 2024; the About page lists modules added through 2024, and Play Store listing was updated 22 Oct 2026. Any workflow note older than 2024 may be stale; the FAQ itself appears current (metadata Nov 2026). Sources: SPNIWCD documents, MWCD About page, Google Play listing (all above).
- [PUBLISHED EVIDENCE][NATIONAL] **"Open API" claim is not API documentation:** WCD's PMMVY FAQ mentions an Open API but provides no specification; Poshan Tracker publishes broad "seamless data sharing" claims with no developer docs. Neither justifies an assumed integration. Sources: MWCD PMMVY page and Poshan Tracker About page (above).
- [UNVERIFIED][NATIONAL] App-store reviews are useful as **risk signals only** (face-matching failures, crashes, no auto-save, overheating) and must not be recorded as confirmed worker workflow or system behaviour. Source: Google Play listing (above).
- [UNKNOWN][NATIONAL] Search coverage for PIB press releases was poor: `pib.gov.in` returned 403 on direct fetch, and one guessed PRID resolved to an unrelated sports release. No PIB release was successfully retrieved; the launch date rests on MWCD-family sources only.
- [UNKNOWN][STATE-AP] No central source retrieved describes AP-specific overrides, and no state-level Poshan Tracker/PMMVY/Bala Sanjeevini documentation was retrieved in Agent 1's scope.
