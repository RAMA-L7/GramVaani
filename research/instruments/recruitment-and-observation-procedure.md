# Recruitment and Observation Procedure (First Workflow Observation)

**Date:** 2026-11-17  
**Status:** Draft procedure — **not authorized for participant contact/observation until required blockers are resolved and evidenced** (see `OUTSTANDING_BLOCKER_REGISTER.md`).  
**Scope:** voluntary Anganwadi workflow observation focused on existing reporting process. **Do not introduce GramVaani/prototype before observing the existing workflow.** No product feature changes, no deployment, no government integration claims.

## 1. Principles

- **Voluntary participation:** no coercion, no pressure, no penalty or disadvantage for declining or stopping. Participation must not affect employment, benefits, supervision, or reporting obligations.
- **No influence on normal process:** explain the study neutrally; do not coach, correct, suggest tools, or demonstrate GramVaani before/during observation of the existing workflow.
- **Minimum necessary, non-identifying data:** anonymized participant/session codes (e.g. W1), no beneficiary/child details, no Aadhaar/IDs, no photos of children/beneficiary screens, no government credentials.
- **Separate consents:** observation/notes consent is separate from **optional** audio recording consent, which is separate from any retained research/evaluation sample consent. Refusal of audio **does not** affect eligibility for observation-only participation (if otherwise consented).
- **Stop anytime:** participant may pause/stop at any time without explanation.

## 2. Recruitment and scheduling (non-coercive)

> **BLOCKER:** recruitment/intermediary approach and contacts must be confirmed as voluntary/non-coercive by responsible owner before any outreach (REC-01). Do not contact workers until authorized.

### 2.1 Approach
- Use a neutral invitation explaining: purpose (understand how reporting is prepared), time/effort, voluntary nature, right to stop, confidentiality/anonymization, and that this is **not** a test of the worker and **not** a government programme.
- If a local intermediary is used, ensure they understand and communicate voluntariness (no pressure, no implication of monitoring/performance consequences). Intermediary role, if any, must be documented and approved.
- Do not over-recruit or offer incentives that could distort consent. Any reimbursement/logistics support (if considered) must be reviewed/approved and described transparently — **not specified/invented here**.

### 2.2 Scheduling script (neutral, to be reviewed/approved)
- "We are learning how reporting work is prepared in day-to-day practice. If you are willing, we would like to observe one reporting task and take notes. This is voluntary, not a test, and will not affect your work. You can stop at any time. We will not ask for child details, Aadhaar, or government app logins."
- Do **not** mention prototype capabilities, voice AI benefits, or expected improvements before observing the existing workflow.

### 2.3 Eligibility/exclusion (apply per approved protocol)
- Include only participants able to consent independently and willing to participate voluntarily.
- Exclude/stop if there is pressure to participate or if real beneficiary/child-identifying information cannot be avoided (reschedule/stop per protocol).

## 3. Consent procedure (separate components)

> **BLOCKERS:** withdrawal contact/path, Telugu human review, legal/ethics review status, and operational consent administration must be resolved/evidenced before participant sessions (CON-01/02/03, GOV-01). Use only approved, reviewed materials.

1. **Before any observation/data collection:** read the approved participant-facing consent script aloud (Telugu, reviewed), provide printed copy, allow questions.
2. **Obtain and document consent separately** for each applicable component:
   - **A. Observation and notes** (required for observation session)
   - **B. Optional audio recording** (only if desired; participant may decline)
   - **C. Retained research/evaluation sample use/storage/sharing beyond immediate session** (only if applicable and separately consented; not assumed)
   - **D. Screen capture/photos of app screens** (optional/rare; no children/PII; prefer not to capture)
3. **Record:** participant/session code, date/time, components consented/refused, and storage location/ref for consent record (separate from research notes/audio).
4. **Re-confirm** if purpose, data handling, or recording method changes.

## 4. Observation-only path (no audio, no prototype)

- If **audio recording consent is declined or not asked**, proceed with **observation and notes only** (if consented) — **no audio capture**. Record refusal neutrally as context/finding, not a failure.
- **No GramVaani introduced** before or during observation of the existing reporting workflow.
- Observe the **complete reporting task**: start at first action needed to prepare the selected report (including paper preparation/gathering/retrieving registers) and finish after required submission, checking, and corrections are complete.
- Time and record: active work, waiting (network/loading), unrelated interruptions separately; assign D-10 categories as active, non-overlapping (count once, note overlap). See `observation-sheet.md` and `session-run-sheet.md`.
- Capture **field labels and process facts** only; never copy beneficiary values, names, identifiers, or credentials. If prohibited/identifying information appears, stop capture, do not retain, follow protocol.

## 5. After observation (and before any prototype discussion, if ever separately consented/approved)

- Debrief respectfully; ask "what did I miss?" per run sheet. Do not evaluate worker.
- If any prototype introduction is considered in a **future, separately approved and consented** activity, it must occur only after observation of existing workflow is complete and under approved protocol — **not part of this observation-only procedure** and not authorized by this document.
- Complete anonymized session note within 24 hours (`note-template.md`), store consent/notes/audio handling per approved procedures.

## 7. Prohibited actions

- No prototype introduction before observing existing workflow.
- No contact/recruitment/observation with participants before authorization and blocker resolution.
- No audio recording/retention without separate explicit consent and approved handling.
- No collection of child/beneficiary PII, Aadhaar, or government credentials.
- No deployment, no government submission/integration claims.
