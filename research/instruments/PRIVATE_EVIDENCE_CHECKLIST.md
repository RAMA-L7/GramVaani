# Private Evidence Checklist (Off-Repo Template)

**Project:** GramVaani (independent personal research project)  
**Date:** 2026-11-17  
**Document type:** **Template/checklist only** — no actual private data, no participant records, no credentials, and no fabricated contacts/approvals may be stored in this file.  
**Purpose:** Track private/off-repo operational evidence required to close governance, consent, safeguarding, data, recruitment, and technical blockers. Link each artifact to blocker IDs in `INDEPENDENT_RESEARCH_GOVERNANCE_EXECUTION_BOARD.md` and `OUTSTANDING_BLOCKER_REGISTER.md`.

## Storage and handling rules (mandatory)

- **Store actual evidence only in approved private/off-repo storage** selected and documented under DATA-02 (e.g. encrypted device/folder or approved restricted system). Do **not** commit to git/public repos.
- **Least privilege:** restrict access to named roles only (Research Lead, Data/Security Owner, Safeguarding Lead/incident owner, Consent Reviewer as needed). Revoke access when no longer required.
- **Separation:** keep consent records, participant linkage, audio/transcripts, and incident records separate where possible; avoid storing identifying details in filenames or locations accessible to unauthorized users.
- **Minimization:** collect/store only what is necessary to evidence closure or operate approved procedures. Do not retain prohibited data (child/beneficiary PII, Aadhaar/IDs, family contacts/addresses, child photos, government credentials/OTPs).
- **Integrity and traceability:** record date, owner, version/source, and verification status for each artifact. Use minimal deletion/access logs without storing deleted sensitive content.
- **No fabrication:** do not create placeholder approvals/contacts/pathways/retention periods as evidence. Mark missing items **UNRESOLVED**.
- **Public vs private:** this template may remain public-safe. Completed checklists containing names, contacts, locations, IDs, or sensitive operational details must be stored privately/off-repo.
