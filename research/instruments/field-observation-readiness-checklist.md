## A. Governance and authorization prerequisites (NOT authorized until evidenced)
*Do not conduct a real worker observation until required items are resolved and evidenced (see `OUTSTANDING_BLOCKER_REGISTER.md`).*

- [ ] **GOV-01/02:** Legal/ethics review status recorded; required pre-pilot/observation sign-offs completed and evidenced.
- [ ] **CON-01/02/03:** Withdrawal contact/path specified and approved; fluent Telugu human review of consent completed and recorded (no linguistic approval claimed until evidenced); retained-sample consent operationalized/approved where applicable.
- [ ] **SAFE-01/02/03:** Safeguarding/field lead and incident owner named; local/district child-protection escalation pathway confirmed; incident notification criteria/contacts/timelines confirmed.
- [ ] **DATA-01/02/03:** Instance-specific retention periods approved; storage locations/systems and access controls specified; deletion owners/tools/timelines assigned.
- [ ] **REC-01:** Recruitment/intermediary approach confirmed voluntary, non-coercive, and authorized.
- [ ] **No-audio path confirmed:** observation-only participation (without audio recording) is available and workable.

## B. Pre-visit preparation
- [ ] **Objective:** observe one complete reporting task end-to-end to produce a workflow map/timing log/actual format-use path (not to demo/test GramVaani).
- [ ] **No GramVaani before observation:** explain study purpose only per consent script; no prototype demo, no product introduction during observation of existing workflow.
- [ ] **Instruments ready/printed:** `consent-script-te.md` (2 copies), `observation-sheet.md`, `session-run-sheet.md`, `workflow-mapping-template.md`, `receiver-checker-verification-questions.md` (only if access/permission allows), `interview-guide.md` (post-observation if used), `note-template.md`.
- [ ] **Protocols reviewed (draft/framework where noted):** `PILOT_SAFEGUARDING_PROTOCOL.md`, recruitment/audio-retention procedures; stop rules, prohibited data, accidental capture, withdrawal/deletion, escalation understood.
- [ ] **Timing rules verified:** **start** = first action to prepare the selected report (incl. paper preparation, gathering information, retrieving registers — not only first app interaction). **finish** = after required submission, checking, and corrections complete.
- [ ] **Measurement rules verified:** record **Active work**, **Waiting (network/loading)**, and **Unrelated interruptions** separately. D-10 categories (A–D) apply to **active work only**, are **non-overlapping** (each active minute assigned to exactly one primary category; count once, note overlap). Waiting/unrelated are not D-10 buckets.
- [ ] **Consent separation ready:** observation/notes consent separate from **optional audio-recording consent** (asked separately) and from any **retained evaluation/research-sample** consent/arrangement (separate, if applicable). Observation consent does not cover audio or retained samples.
- [ ] **Anonymization/no PII:** participant code assigned (e.g. W__); signed consent stored separately from notes (never photographed). No beneficiary/child data, no names, no centre names/addresses, no Aadhaar/IDs, no phone numbers, no credentials, no identifying photos/screenshots/values.
- [ ] **Synthetic-only practice:** any rehearsal/training uses synthetic/non-identifying data only.
- [ ] **Kit:** stopwatch/clock, pen, printed sheets, phone silent; audio device only if separately consented and per approved protocol. No irrelevant photos.

## C. During visit (conduct)
- [ ] Read consent script aloud; confirm voluntary participation and right to stop/skip anytime. Record consent components separately.
- [ ] **If audio refused:** proceed with observation-only (no audio) and note neutrally.
- [ ] **Observe existing workflow first** (no GramVaani introduced). Time the complete task per definitions above.
- [ ] Record steps chronologically `[obs]/[said]`; capture field labels/artifacts verbatim; **never copy beneficiary values or identifying details**.
- [ ] Track active/waiting/unrelated interruptions separately; assign D-10 without double-counting (overlap noted, counted once).
- [ ] **Receiver/checker verification (only if access and permission allow):** ask as verification questions after/alongside observation as appropriate; mark responses `[said]`, not assumptions (use `receiver-checker-verification-questions.md`). If not permitted/observed, record "not observed."
- [ ] If prohibited/identifying information appears: stop relevant capture, do not retain/copy, follow protocol.
- [ ] Optional speech sample / retained evaluation recording: **only if separately consented and arranged**; not assumed from observation consent.

## D. After visit (within 24–48 h)
- [ ] Complete anonymized note/workflow map (`note-template.md`, `workflow-mapping-template.md`) within 24 hours; keep `[obs]/[said]` separate and mark unverified items.
- [ ] Store consent/permission records separately from notes (restricted location). Record audio handling/deletion status if applicable (per approved procedure).
- [ ] Promote observations to `research/RESEARCH_FINDINGS.md` with evidence labels as appropriate; update gaps/assumptions; do not overclaim.
- [ ] Note follow-ups/instrument changes; document what remains unverified.

## Quick reminders (do / don't)
- **Do:** observe first; time start→finish incl. submission/checking/corrections; separate active/waiting/unrelated; count D-10 once; use codes only; support "no audio" participation.
- **Don't:** introduce GramVaani before observation; assume permissions; copy PII/values; treat verification answers as assumptions; claim authorization/approvals without evidence.

*This checklist is a readiness aid, not legal/ethics approval.*
