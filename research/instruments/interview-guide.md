# Interview guide — Anganwadi reporting

*Semi-structured, 30–40 minutes, after the observation session. Telugu. Ask, then be quiet.*

---

## Before you start
- Consent read aloud and signed. Recording consent asked separately.
- Reminder: "This is not a test of you. I am trying to understand the work, not judge it."
- No beneficiary details. If they start naming a child or family, say: "no names needed — just the pattern."

## 1. Warm-up (3 min)
1. How long have you been working at this centre?
2. Walk me through a normal day, from opening to closing. (Let them talk; note where reporting appears.)

## 2. The reporting task (12 min)
3. Yesterday, when did you sit down to enter the report? What had to be finished first?
4. Show me the app you use. *(Observe only; do not ask for logins.)*
5. Which parts do you type, and which parts do you tap or select?
6. Which part takes the longest? Why do you think that is?
7. Do you ever have to go back and check something before entering it? What do you check, and where?
8. What happens if you enter something wrong? How would you notice? How would you fix it?

## 3. Language (6 min)
9. When you talk about this work with another worker, how do you say it? Give me an example sentence.
10. Which words do you always say in English, even in Telugu? *(attendance, register, growth chart…)*
11. If you had to say today's report out loud to your supervisor, what would you say? *(Let them speak
    uninterrupted. This is the single most valuable 60 seconds of the session.)*

## 4. Workarounds and effort (6 min)
12. Do you write anything on paper first? Why?
13. Do you ever prepare the report at home, or after hours? How often?
14. What do you do when you are tired or in a hurry?
15. If you could remove one step from reporting, which one?

## 5. The voice idea — reactions (6 min)
*Describe, don't demo.* "Imagine you speak the report in Telugu, see the text, fix a word or two, and it
fills the report."
16. What do you like about that? What worries you?
17. What would you not want to say out loud? *(privacy, noise, who is nearby)*
18. Would you trust the text if you could read it before it is used? What would you check?
19. Is there anything it must never do?

## 6. Card sort (5 min)
Hand over the printed candidate field list. Ask: **must have / sometimes / not needed**, and "what is
missing from this list?" Photograph the piles. Note every added item verbatim.

## 7. Close (2 min)
20. What should I have asked and didn't?
21. May I come back with a rough version to try?

---

## Note-taking rules
- Quote, don't paraphrase, for language examples — Telugu in Telugu script, plus a rough English gloss.
- Mark each answer `[obs]` if you saw it, `[said]` if they told you.
- Time the reporting steps on the observation sheet; don't rely on their estimate.
- Write the note within 24 hours, or the detail is gone.
