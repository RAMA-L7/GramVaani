# Receiver/checker verification question set

**Purpose:** verify how a completed report is received, checked, and accepted — to complement (not replace) workflow observation.  
**Status:** **Verification questions only, not assumptions.** Record answers as reported `[said]`, and mark anything not confirmed as "not verified/not observed." Do not treat responses as official requirements without authorization/evidence.

## When and how to use (permissions and sequencing)
- **Use only where access and permission allow.** Confirm appropriate authorization and participant/intermediary permission before engaging a receiver/checker (see `OUTSTANDING_BLOCKER_REGISTER.md`, safeguarding/recruitment procedures). This is not authorized until evidenced.
- **Sequencing:** conduct **after or alongside workflow observation as appropriate**. Do not introduce GramVaani or a prototype before observing the existing workflow. Keep receiver/checker verification separate from observation of the worker's task unless the setting and permissions explicitly allow combined observation.
- **Consent/voluntariness:** explain purpose, voluntary participation, and right to stop/skip questions. No pressure; no incentives that distort participation. Record consent/permission per applicable protocol.
- **Anonymization:** use role/participant codes only. No beneficiary/child data, no names, no centre identifiers/addresses, no Aadhaar/IDs, no phone numbers, no credentials. Do not request or record sensitive values; ask about process, formats, and expectations.
- **Evidence labeling:** tag each response `[said]` with date/context code; note if corroborated by observation `[obs]` in a separate session. Capture verbatim phrasing where useful (non-identifying).

## 1. Receiver and role (who receives the report)
1. Who receives this report after it is prepared? (role/title, not personal name) `[said]`
2. Is the same person/role always the receiver, or does it vary (by report type, day, location, absence)? `[said]`
3. When and how is the report handed over or made available (in person, digital submission, message, other)? `[said]`
4. Is there a different person/role who checks/verifies it than the one who receives it? Who? `[said]`
5. At what point in the process do they receive/check it (immediately after preparation, at submission, later batch)? `[said]`

## 2. Expected format and channel
6. In what format is the report expected (paper register, app/portal entry, photo, spreadsheet, message, printed copy, other)? `[said]`
7. Is a specific template, form, field order, or labeling required? What makes a submission "in the right format"? `[said]`
8. If both paper and digital exist, which is the expected/authoritative format for receipt/checking? `[said]`
9. Are there timing/cadence expectations for when it must reach the receiver/checker? `[said]`

## 3. Required vs optional information
10. What information/fields are **required** for the report to be accepted? `[said]`
11. What information/fields are **optional** or situational? `[said]`
12. Are there minimum completeness checks (e.g. totals present, signatures/marks, attachments)? `[said]`
13. How do you know if something required is missing at the time of receipt/checking? `[said]`

## 4. Duplicate entry across systems
14. Is the same information entered or submitted in more than one place/system/register? Which ones? `[said]`
15. If duplication exists: which version is checked first, and which is treated as authoritative for acceptance? `[said]`
16. Do you compare entries across systems/registers? What mismatches matter? `[said]`
17. Is any copying/re-entry expected from the worker after initial preparation? `[said]`

## 5. When incorrect or incomplete
18. What happens if the report is **incorrect** (wrong value/total/entry)? How is it detected? `[said]`
19. What happens if the report is **incomplete** (missing required information)? `[said]`
20. Who corrects it, and how (worker revises, receiver edits, re-submission, annotation)? `[said]`
21. What is the timeline/expectation for correction and re-submission? `[said]`
22. What are common reasons reports are returned or need correction? `[said]` *(process reasons only; no individual cases/PII)*

## 6. Work remaining after preparation
23. After the worker says the report is "prepared," what work remains before it is considered submitted/accepted? `[said]`
24. Are there steps the worker must do after submission (confirmation, follow-up, responding to queries, corrections)? `[said]`
25. Are there steps the receiver/checker must do after receipt (verification, consolidation, forwarding, filing)? `[said]`
26. Does waiting (e.g. network, availability, approvals) occur after preparation? Who bears it? `[said]`

## 7. Acceptance and verification steps
27. What checks are performed to verify/accept the report (completeness, totals, consistency, signatures, supporting artifacts)? `[said]`
28. How is acceptance recorded or communicated back (acknowledgment, mark/signature, system status, message)? `[said]`
29. What makes a report definitively "accepted/complete" vs "pending/corrected"? `[said]`
30. If a report cannot be accepted the same day, what happens next? `[said]`

---

## Closing and documentation
31. Is there anything about receiving/checking this report that I did not ask but matters? `[said]`
32. Are there differences across report types or situations I should be aware of? `[said]`

**After the conversation (within 24 h):**
- Record responses in an anonymized note with participant/role code, date, and `[said]` labels; separate from consent/permission records.
- Note what was **not verified/not observed** and any contradictions with observed workflow (without resolving by assumption).
- If any prohibited/identifying information was shared inadvertently: do not retain it; follow safeguarding/data-handling protocol.
- Do not introduce GramVaani, make product claims, or infer official requirements from answers. Keep findings as reported evidence pending corroboration.
