# Session notes

One file per session: `YYYY-MM-DD-session-NN.md`, from
[`../instruments/note-template.md`](../instruments/note-template.md).

## Rules

- **Anonymised codes only** (`W1`, `S1`). No worker names, no centre names, no child names.
- **No beneficiary data**, no credentials, no screenshots of beneficiary screens.
- **Write within 24 hours.** After that, the detail is gone and the note becomes fiction.
- **Separate facts from interpretation.** Template §2 is what happened; §7 is what you think it means.
- **Raw notes are not committed** if they contain any identifying reference — use the `.private.md` suffix,
  which `.gitignore` already excludes.

## Naming

| File | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `2026-11-20-session-01.md` | First session, 20 Nov 2026 |
| `2026-11-20-session-01.private.md` | Same session, raw notes that must stay local |
| `2026-11-22-supervisor-01.md` | Supervisor interview |

## Retention

Audio and raw notes are deleted 90 days after the study closes, or immediately on request. Summaries — the
kind that belong in `RESEARCH_FINDINGS.md` — are the durable artefact.

## Empty is honest

A note that says "the worker declined to be recorded, here is what I observed" is more valuable than a
reconstructed transcript. Do not fill gaps from memory.
