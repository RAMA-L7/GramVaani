_Multi-agent study | All sources accessed 2026-11-16 | Compiled by lead research agent_

This is the deduplicated source register for all seven domain research files. Duplicate URLs from
different domain files were merged; the **Used in** column shows which files cite each source.
Publication dates are transcribed as reported by the domain agents; "not stated" means the source did
not display a date. **Check** records whether the independent verification agent re-fetched the source.

## Method and caveats

- The built-in `websearch` integration failed throughout (HTTP 402/429). Agents used direct `webfetch`,
  arXiv API, publisher sites and news RSS. This is a real coverage limitation, not a neutral detail.
- The verification agent attempted 20 fetches (17 succeeded, 3 blocked). **CONFIRMED** means the cited
  content was independently re-read and matched the claim. It does not mean peer-reviewed.
- **BLOCKED** sources (Gazette PDFs, some PIB pages, Indian government domains returning 403) are retained
  for traceability but must not be used as sole support for a decision.
- Numbering is stable within this document. Domain files keep their own local source numbers.
