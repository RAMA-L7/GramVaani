# Andhra Pradesh Workflow Research — Bala Sanjeevini and state systems
_Agent 2 | Access date: 2026-11-16 | Status: research notes, no application changes_

## 1. Scope and method (include search languages and limitations)

**Scope.** Andhra Pradesh (AP) only, except where a national scheme frame is needed to interpret an AP page. Every substantive finding below is prefixed with exactly one label: `[VERIFIED]` (2+ independent credible sources, both listed), `[PUBLISHED EVIDENCE]` (one credible published/official source), `[UNVERIFIED]` (inference, marketing, participant claim, AI summary, or third-party app-store metadata), or `[UNKNOWN]` (searched, not established). Scope is marked `[STATE-AP]` or `[NATIONAL]`.

**Languages.** English and Telugu (తెలుగు). Telugu queries used include బాల సంజీవిని, బాల సంజీవని యాప్ ఫోటోలు అప్ లోడ్, అంగన్ వాడీ కార్యకలాపాలు ఫోటోలు, and నేరుగా తెలుగు వార్తా సైట్లు (ETV Bharat, Eenadu). Telugu reporting was essential: it is where the photo-capture/FRS mandate and the 2026 supply story were found.

**Method.** Direct fetches of official AP portals and Google Play, plus search via DuckDuckGo HTML, Yahoo Search (through the Jina reader), and Google News RSS. Findings were cross-checked across official pages, mainstream press, and app-store metadata. No login was attempted, no private account or beneficiary data was accessed, and no API/field/integration was inferred from app-store copy or participant statements.

**Limitations and search failures (important).**
- The built-in `websearch` tool failed throughout with HTTP 402/429; Google web search returned an "unusual traffic" captcha; Startpage, Mojeek, Brave and (after the first queries) DuckDuckGo returned bot challenges or 429. Yahoo Search via `r.jina.ai` and Google News RSS were the working search paths.
- Google News RSS article links are opaque redirects; direct resolution failed. Publisher URLs were recovered through Yahoo instead.
- `r.jina.ai` returned 403/422 for some targets (notably the Bala Sanjeevani privacy policy and some Google News links); those were fetched directly instead.
- **No official Bala Sanjeevani / 2.0 user manual, SOP, circular, tender, or training PDF was found** in any search, nor any official page listing app screens/fields.
- Yahoo's "AI Summary / Scout" answers were generated text (e.g. claiming an April 2025 launch and generic API/SSO capability). These are explicitly excluded from evidence.
- App-store descriptions are treated as developer-published marketing, not as proof of a workflow. No app was installed or inspected.

## 2. Bala Sanjeevini / 2.0 — official picture

### 2.1 Ownership and purpose

- `[VERIFIED] [STATE-AP]` **"Bala Sanjeevani" is both a state supplementary-nutrition scheme and the name of a departmental digital system/app.** The AP WCDA&SC department portal describes "Supplementary Nutrition Programme- Balasanjeevani" with two variants: **Balasanjeevani Tribal** (77 Tribal Scheduled and Sub-Plan mandals) and **Balasanjeevani Plain** (remaining mandals, 47,287 Anganwadi Centres). Sources: WCDASC "Women development & Child Welfare" page (S7) and WDCW "Saksham Anganwadi and Poshan 2.0" page (S5, which lists "Nutrition Kit" food items — ragi flour, jaggery, groundnut chikki, dry dates — for pregnant/lactating women). A June 2026 Telugu news report independently describes "Bala Sanjeevani kits" containing ragi flour, puffed rice, chikki, jaggery and dried dates distributed to pregnant women and lactating mothers (S10).
- `[VERIFIED] [STATE-AP]` **Owner/developer:** Department of Women Development & Child Welfare (WD&CW), Government of Andhra Pradesh; the official site footer reads "Department of Women Development & Child Welfare, Govt of A.P." and "Designed, Developed & Maintained by TCS" (Tata Consultancy Services) (S1, S3). The Google Play developer is "AP WDCW", with support address "COMMISSIONERATE OF WOMEN DEVELOPMENT AND CHILD WELFARE, Jampani Towers, Amaravathi Road, Guntur" (S2). The WCDA&SC portal carries the same TCS footer (S7).
- `[PUBLISHED EVIDENCE] [STATE-AP]` **Stated purpose** on the official portal: reduce anaemia among pregnant and lactating women and reduce malnutrition among children under five through 55,607 Anganwadi Centres in 257 ICDS Projects; the app listing adds delivery of "Nutrition, Health, and preschool services" (S1, S2).
- `[PUBLISHED EVIDENCE] [STATE-AP]` **Historical depth:** a 2019 report documents a "Modified Bala Sanjeevani Scheme" being reintroduced in Chittoor district at 4,768 anganwadi centres from January [2020] with a modified menu (S11). This establishes that the *scheme name* predates the 2.0 app by years; it is not evidence about app history.
- `[VERIFIED] [STATE-AP]` **Parallel distinct app identity:** Google Play lists both "Bala Sanjeevani 2.0" (`com.ap.wdcw`, 100K+ downloads, developer listing "Women Development and Child Welfare Department – SNP Services") and an older separate app "Bala Sanjeevani – WDCW" (`com.ap.wdcw.bs`, 1K+ downloads, "Women Development and Child Welfare Department – ICDS Services") (S2, S12). They are published as two distinct packages under the same department.

### 2.2 Modules and workflows

- `[PUBLISHED EVIDENCE] [STATE-AP]` The official portal presents **three service cards**: Supplementary Nutrition (pregnant women, lactating mothers, children below 6; "service provided by Anganwadi Workers and Helpers"), Pre School Education (children 3–6, "service provided by Anganwadi Workers"), and Growth Monitoring (children 0–5, "to track the malnourished status of children") (S1).
- `[PUBLISHED EVIDENCE] [STATE-AP]` The 2.0 store listing says the app covers "Nutrition, Health, and preschool services" across 257 projects in 26 districts and 55,607 AWCs; the most recent release note is "Added date validations for other commodity receipts" (S2, updated 2026-11-04). This is the only module hint available and is **developer marketing metadata, not an SOP** — it suggests a commodity-receipt recording function but does not establish field names or workflow steps.
- `[PUBLISHED EVIDENCE] [STATE-AP]` The WDCW portal lists **Bala Sanjeevani as one entry among separate systems** — Poshan Tracker, Bala Sanjeevani, MIS/eSadhana, Milk Supply Tracking System, THR-FRS, Supervisor's Supervision, AWW/AWH Recruitment System (S6). No page describes them as a single integrated product.
- `[UNVERIFIED] [STATE-AP]` A third-party APK mirror states version 2.0.0 was released 9 Apr 2025 (89.7 MB) (S14); AppBrain lists the app from 17 Jul 2024 (S15). These are app-store/mirror metadata. **No official launch date for 2.0 was found** — `[UNKNOWN]`.
- `[UNKNOWN] [STATE-AP]` No official document describing Bala Sanjeevini's internal modules, menus, user roles, data fields, reporting formats, or SOP was found. Do not infer these from screenshots or app-store text.

### 2.3 Documentation and current status

- `[PUBLISHED EVIDENCE] [STATE-AP]` Official web presence: `balasanjeevani.ap.gov.in/BS/Default.aspx`, with a login (username, password, CAPTCHA) and links to Organogram, Dashboards and Reports; the "Reports"/"Dashboards" navigation items on the fetched page had no resolvable destinations (S1). Footer contact: Jampani Towers, Amaravathi Road, Guntur – 522006; phone 0866-2332587 (the 2.0 site) / 0863-2332587 (the WDCW portal) (S1, S4).
- `[PUBLISHED EVIDENCE] [STATE-AP]` Privacy policy: "Women Development & Child Welfare Department does not automatically capture any specific personal information…"; "We do not sell or share any personally identifiable information volunteered on Bala Sanjeevani 2.0 to any third party (public/private)." Cookie types: analytics, service, non-persistent. Maintained by TCS (S3). **This is a website privacy statement, not evidence about system-to-system data exchange with Poshan Tracker.**
- `[PUBLISHED EVIDENCE] [STATE-AP]` **Active as of late 2026:** the 2.0 app was updated 4 Nov 2026 (S2), the older `com.ap.wdcw.bs` app 20 Aug 2026 (S12), and the Supervision app 14 Nov 2026 (S13). Google Play reports ~100K+ downloads for 2.0 (S2).
- `[PUBLISHED EVIDENCE] [STATE-AP]` **June 2026 operational problem:** a Telugu report says Bala Sanjeevani kit supplies were delayed in six Rayalaseema districts (14,096 AWCs); three districts had received no kits by the reporting Friday; four contractors supply the state; last month's notices to the contractor did not fix it (S10). Distribution is scheduled for the first and third Friday each month (S10; consistent with S5). This concerns the physical scheme, not the app.
- `[UNVERIFIED] [STATE-AP]` A Telugu YouTube video titled "How to reset the password in the Bala Sanjeevini app" exists (S16), indicating informal Telugu-language help content; uploader and official status could not be verified from the fetched page. Treat as community help, not an official manual.
- `[UNKNOWN] [STATE-AP]` Official manuals, SOPs, circulars, or training material specifically for Bala Sanjeevini 2.0 were not found despite targeted English and Telugu searches.

## 3. AP WDCW — official resources

- `[PUBLISHED EVIDENCE] [STATE-AP]` **Official site:** `wdcw.ap.gov.in`. It states 257 ICDS Projects, 55,776 Anganwadis across 28 districts, three flagship missions, and lists department functions including "Implementation of Integrated Child Development Services Scheme and capacity building of Anganwadi workers and helpers", "Monitoring", "Reporting", "Fund Releases", and "Issuing Guidelines" (S4). RTI 4(1)(b)(i) and PIO documents are linked; not parsed in this study.
- `[PUBLISHED EVIDENCE] [STATE-AP]` **Saksham Anganwadi & Poshan 2.0 page** (S5): coverage 257 projects, 2,205 sectors, 55,776 AWCs, 8,459 tribal AWCs, 4.4 lakh pregnant & lactating women, 11.4 lakh children 7m–3y, 7.3 lakh children 3–6y, 23.1 lakh total beneficiaries. Services: Supplementary Nutrition, Nutrition & Health Education, Preschool Education, Immunization, Health Check-ups, Referrals. Two nutrition/health days per month (1st Friday: growth monitoring & THR; 3rd Friday: immunization/ANC by ANM and counselling) (S5).
- `[PUBLISHED EVIDENCE] [STATE-AP]` **Preschool/ECCE resources documented on the same official page** (S5): age-specific teaching-learning material worth ₹6.78 Cr developed with SCERT; a ₹5 Cr Pre-School Education Kit of **19 items with a user manual** supplied to all AWCs; monthly and daily activity calendars; PP-1/PP-2 workbooks and activity books; **Remote Learning Messages (RLMs) shared with all AWWs**; 12 spoken-English videos for AWWs made with SCERT; 25 Foundational Learning and Numeracy videos on the department YouTube channel; bilingual story books; Mother's Groups "sharing the pre-school activities daily" to engage children at home; monthly ECCE meeting with parents. Training: "Poshan Bhi Padai Bhi" two-tier training (1,128 CDPOs/Supervisors/ECCE experts and 55,776 AWWs); SALT/World Bank 120-day FLN certificate course; 41 Model Foundation Schools (training 11–15 Mar 2024); UNICEF 8.5-hour online ECCE certificate course, 51,283 (90.63%) completed as of 15 May 2024; 1,038 Hub Anganwadi Centres in 13 districts supported by ITC & Pratham; 78 CDPOs trained in leadership 20–22 Dec 2022 (S5).
- `[PUBLISHED EVIDENCE] [STATE-AP]` **Digital systems listed by the department** (S6): Poshan Tracker (`poshantracker.in`), Bala Sanjeevani (`balasanjeevani.ap.gov.in`), eSadhana MIS (`mis.wdcw.ap.gov.in`), Milk Supply Tracking System (`apddcf.ap.gov.in/icdcsp`), THR-FRS (`wdcwfrs.ap.gov.in`), Supervisor's Supervision (`wdcwss.ap.gov.in/dashboard-1/`), and AWW/AWH Recruitment System. Separately, the WCDA&SC portal (S7) says "Real Time Monitoring of ICDS services through Poshan Tracker application" provides a 360-degree view of AWC activities, AWW service delivery and beneficiary management.
- `[PUBLISHED EVIDENCE] [STATE-AP]` **WCDA&SC portal** (`wcdsc.ap.gov.in/WCDASC/Public/WDCW.aspx`, S7) documents state schemes and infrastructure: Saksham Anganwadis to provide better infrastructure **including internet/Wi-Fi connectivity and LED screens**; 55,607 AWCs in 257 projects, of which 21,484 in own buildings, 5,757 in school classrooms, 4,823 in rent-free accommodation, 23,543 rented.
- `[UNVERIFIED] [STATE-AP]` A user-uploaded document titled "Bala Sanjeevani THR Report 2024" appears on Scribd, with beneficiary counts for eggs/milk/Balamrutham by AWC code. It is not an official publication and was not used to define fields or formats.
- `[UNKNOWN] [STATE-AP]` No official circular mandating a specific photo/evidence format for preschool reporting was located on WDCW/WCDASC.

## 4. Preschool activity reporting and photo submission

- `[VERIFIED] [STATE-AP]` **Photo capture is mandated for commodity distribution, not for preschool activities.** Two independent news sources describe a requirement that beneficiaries receive commodities only after photo capture, OTP and FRS (The Hindu, 5 Aug 2025, S9) and that "once a photo is taken in the apps, commodity details appear" with an SMS to the beneficiary (ETV Bharat, 17 Jul 2025, S8). Both name Poshan Tracker and Bala Sanjeevani together. **This is face/beneficiary identification for ration distribution — it must not be generalised into a preschool-photo reporting rule.**
- `[PUBLISHED EVIDENCE] [STATE-AP]` The official WDCW Saksham page documents preschool *activities* (daily/monthly calendars, RLMs, workbooks, Mother's Groups sharing activities daily, monthly ECCE meetings with parents) but **does not state any requirement to photograph, upload, or submit preschool activity evidence** (S5).
- `[UNVERIFIED] [STATE-AP]` A Facebook post by "WCD Andhra Pradesh" dated 10 Jan 2019 is indexed with text referring to "Pre school activities and sreemantham and bala sanjeevani distribution… in ICDS Project, Kurnool urban" (S17, search-result snippet only; the post itself could not be opened). At most this shows the department publicises such activities; it is not a reporting rule.
- `[UNKNOWN] [STATE-AP]` **No official or credible published requirement to photograph preschool activities, upload such photos, or submit them as evidence to WDCW/Bala Sanjeevini was found**, in English or Telugu. If a field worker reports such a practice, record it as local/supervisor-level instruction `[UNVERIFIED]` until a circular or form is produced.
- `[UNVERIFIED] [STATE-AP]` Google Play data-safety declarations: the **Supervision App WDCW AP** (`com.entrolabs.wdcw`) declares collection of "Location, Photos and videos, and Device or other IDs", while Bala Sanjeevani 2.0 and the older app declare "Location, Personal info and 2 others"; all three declare "No data shared with third parties", "Data isn't encrypted", "Data can't be deleted" (S2, S12, S13). The Supervision App is a *supervisor* monitoring tool ("developed to monitor service delivery at AWC", S13) — do not attribute its photo capability to AWW preschool reporting.

## 5. Poshan Tracker ↔ Bala Sanjeevini relationship — evidence check

- `[UNKNOWN] [STATE-AP]` **No official documentation of integration found.** Searches of government portals (`site:ap.gov.in`), app listings, news, and Telugu sources found **no** government order, tender, press release, audit report, academic study, API specification, SSO arrangement, shared-database description, or data-synchronisation statement connecting Poshan Tracker and Bala Sanjeevini. Nothing here establishes synchronisation, single sign-on, or a shared backend.
- `[VERIFIED] [STATE-AP]` What *is* documented is **parallel, separately-listed use**: the WDCW MIS page lists Poshan Tracker and Bala Sanjeevani as two separate tiles with different URLs (S6); the WCDA&SC page describes Poshan Tracker as the real-time ICDS monitoring application (S7); and two independent news reports describe AWWs using both apps in the same distribution process (S8, S9). Parallel use ≠ integration.
- `[PUBLISHED EVIDENCE] [STATE-AP]` Attribution in reporting is split: ETV Bharat (2025) says the AP government brought Bala Sanjeevani and the central government brought Poshan Tracker (S8); The Hindu (2025) refers to "Poshan and Bala Sanjeevini Apps" together without describing any data flow (S9). Neither documents a technical link.
- `[UNVERIFIED] [STATE-AP]` A Google Play user review (13 Nov 2026) complains that after "Poshan tracker app lo Migration" workers stay in that app for months rather than Bala Sanjeevini (S2). This is a participant claim and may refer to workflow migration, not data migration; do not use it as integration evidence.
- `[UNVERIFIED] [STATE-AP]` Yahoo/Scout AI summaries asserting that these platforms "utilize APIs" and "facilitate single sign-on" are generated text with no cited source and are explicitly rejected as evidence.

## 7. Contradictions, outdated documentation and cautions

- `[VERIFIED] [STATE-AP]` **Conflicting AWC/district counts:** Bala Sanjeevani site, 2.0 Play listing and WCDASC page say **55,607 AWCs in 26 districts** (48,770 main + 6,837 mini; 257 projects: 200 rural, 33 urban, 24 tribal) (S1, S2, S7). The newer WDCW portal says **55,776 Anganwadis across 28 districts** and its Saksham page says 55,776 AWCs / 8,459 tribal AWCs (S4, S5). Treat all as time-varying administrative figures; do not treat either as current truth without a date.
- `[PUBLISHED EVIDENCE] [STATE-AP]` **Conflicting district counts within the same department** (26 vs 28) is an outdated/documentation-drift signal; the WDCW portal is © 2026 and its 2,205 sectors / ₹ figures suggest newer content, but no publication date is attached to the numbers.
- `[PUBLISHED EVIDENCE] [STATE-AP]` **Scheme vs app ambiguity:** "Bala Sanjeevani" denotes the nutrition scheme/kit in official pages and 2026 news (S7, S10), while "Bala Sanjeevani 2.0" is the specific app package (S2). Do not conflate them in claims about "what workers do".
- `[UNVERIFIED] [STATE-AP]` **Do not treat app-store copy as workflow proof.** "SNP Services" / "ICDS Services" developer labels, "date validations for other commodity receipts", privacy/data-safety declarations and download counts describe the product and its marketing, not a mandated field procedure (S2, S12, S13).
- `[UNVERIFIED] [STATE-AP]` **Do not treat AI summaries as sources.** Yahoo Scout summaries about launch dates and API/SSO were generated and unsourced.
- `[PUBLISHED EVIDENCE] [STATE-AP]` **Timing caveat:** the strongest workflow descriptions of photo/OTP/FRS come from July–August 2025 (S8, S9). They may not reflect 2026 practice; the 2026 evidence found concerns supply delays and app update activity only (S2, S10).
- `[PUBLISHED EVIDENCE] [STATE-AP]` **Data-protection caution for the prototype:** Google Play declarations for all three department apps state data is not encrypted and cannot be deleted (S2, S12, S13), while the site privacy policy says PII is not shared with third parties (S3). These are developer declarations, not a security audit. GramVaani must keep its research prototype free of beneficiary data and official-compatibility claims.
