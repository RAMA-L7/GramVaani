## Reading rules

1. Every finding in the domain files carries `[VERIFIED]`, `[PUBLISHED EVIDENCE]`, `[UNVERIFIED]` or
   `[UNKNOWN]`, plus `[NATIONAL]` / `[STATE-AP]` where relevant.
2. Two documents from one ministry, department or encyclopedia are **one source**, not independent
   corroboration. Reviewer corrections are recorded in RESEARCH_GAPS §3.
3. App-store descriptions and vendor marketing are never proof of a worker's actual workflow.
4. Nothing here authorises an integration, an API call, a submission, beneficiary data or an official claim.
5. The built-in `websearch` tool failed during the study (HTTP 402/429); see source-quality caveats in
   SOURCES_AND_REFERENCES.md.
